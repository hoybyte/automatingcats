I've spent a lot of time learning new technologies,
experimenting with software, and solving problems.

Sometimes things work immediately.

Other times, I spend hours troubleshooting something
that turns out to be a simple configuration error.

## Why I'm Writing

I wanted a place to document those experiences.

Not just the solutions, but also the process of
figuring things out.

I also wanted somewhere to write about things
outside technology.

## What You'll Find Here

I'll be writing about:

- Learning Python and software development.
- Building applications and automation tools.
- Linux, Docker, and my home lab.
- Lessons learned from troubleshooting.
- Thoughts on life and personal growth.

## The Journey Begins

I don't expect to have all the answers.

I simply want to keep learning, building, and
documenting what happens along the way.
